AI was supposed to make engineering teams faster, but instead, it has created a new kind of bottleneck. Developers are generating code at unprecedented speed, while senior engineers are overwhelmed by the volume and complexity of reviews. The result is a system that moves faster on the surface, but struggles to convert that speed into meaningful, high-quality value.

The real issue isn't productivity, it's flow. When AI accelerates output without rethinking how work is validated, integrated, and governed, it amplifies existing constraints rather than removing them. Teams become trapped in cycles of rework, review fatigue, and decision delays.

The fix isn't typing faster, it's shifting from passive AI tools to autonomous, well-orchestrated AI workflows. In this session, we'll explore how high-performing teams are designing Agentic Workflows that don't just assist, but actively execute within clearly defined boundaries. You'll see how to distribute work between humans and machines in a way that preserves quality, improves flow, and reduces cognitive load.

This session breaks down how to give AI the "hands" to handle routine and repeatable tasks, while implementing the governance, observability, and security guardrails required for enterprise environments. This includes practical patterns for trust, control, and accountability in AI-driven delivery systems.

You'll walk away with a real-world playbook for orchestrating AI at scale, along with a clear understanding of how the traditional Scrum Master role is evolving into the "Architect of Intent", a leader who designs systems of work, aligns human and machine capabilities, and creates the conditions for teams to focus on what humans do best: judgment, creativity, and collaboration.
