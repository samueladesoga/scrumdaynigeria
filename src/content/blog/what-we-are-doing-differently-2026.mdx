At Scrum Day Nigeria 2026, we're not just hosting another conference — we're shaping a movement. A movement to rediscover the essence of the Scrum Framework and reimagine how it empowers teams to deliver better products — sooner, safer, and happier.

This year, our focus goes beyond sharing knowledge; it's about building a stronger, more connected Agile community across Nigeria and Africa. That's why we're intentionally spotlighting Nigerian and African experts and practitioners — the everyday champions who are redefining collaboration, leadership, and product delivery within their organisations.

We believe that the most meaningful change begins within our communities. Scrum Day Nigeria 2026 will be a space for reflection, reinvention, and reawakening — a moment to pause, share, and collectively chart the path forward for agility in Africa.

Together, we'll learn, unlearn, and co-create the future of work — one conversation, one product, and one sprint at a time.
