It's been an impactful day so far, but my biggest takeaway is a point made by one of the panelists, Ravi: 'Shift left.' This means that when you have processes involved in product management, you shouldn't wait until it's time for QA or testing. You should shift everything left and, as much as possible, put things and policies in place from the beginning to avoid mishaps later on.

Another key takeaway for me is the potential impact of AI in product development. There was a great panel session on that as well.

This is my first Scrum Day, and I'm glad I attended. I didn't come last year, and I'm sure I missed out on a lot, so I'm already looking forward to next year's event.
